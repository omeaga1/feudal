How it works

  1. 1

    Link your profile once

    Search your name and pick your AoE4 World profile. That's the only setup.

  2. 2

    Queue up like normal

    Leave Feudal open on a second screen or in the background. It checks for a new match every 15 seconds.

  3. 3

    Read the brief on the loading screen

    Both civs are detected and the brief opens on its own. Glance at it, then go play.

It never touches the game.

Feudal doesn't read game memory, game files, network traffic or your screen. It can't trip anti-cheat or put a ranked account at risk. Everything it knows about a live match comes from the public AoE4 World API, the same data on your loading screen and public profile.

Questions

Can this get me banned?

Feudal has no connection to the game at all. It's a separate window that reads public match data from AoE4 World, the same way the AoE4 World website does. There's nothing for anti-cheat to see.

Which games does it detect?

Ranked and quick-match games, which AoE4 World tracks. For custom lobbies or games against AI, use the matchup browser and pick both civs yourself.

Does it work mid-game?

It's built for the loading screen. The brief appears on its own and doesn't need clicks during a match, so you can keep your hands on the game.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no paid tier. Microsoft's content rules require fan tools like this to stay free.

Windows says the installer is from an unknown publisher.

Feudal isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". The installer is built in the open by GitHub Actions from the source code.
